# Physics audit — known parameters vs known results (2026-07-22)

Reproducible: `python verify/audit_physics.py` (23/23 quantitative items PASS,
2 documented notes). Unlike the regression suite (internal second methods),
every item checks against an EXTERNAL published or documented number.

## What was checked and what came out

1. **Foundation F1b check triplet reproduced exactly**: at (Δ=5.4, Γ=6.5,
   w_half=Γ/2), the exact closed form gives 0.3979/0.3929/0.3877 at
   μ = 0.05/0.3/1.0 — matching the foundation doc's published check values to
   all quoted digits (the doc's MC agreed within its error bars).
2. **F1a convention equivalence**: the doc's half-width formulas and the code's
   full-width forms agree to 1e-12.
3. **F7 table reproduced** (all ε_min rows within 0.015).
4. **Theorem-0 saturated value ½** and the textbook two-emitter g² = 0.5
   (the threshold OE2008 itself quotes).
5. **Reischle background corrections**: F2 inversion returns 0.038/0.032 vs
   the paper's published 0.04/0.03.
6. **Varshni GaAs vs digitized Chatzarakis X positions** (not fitted): worst
   residual 3.2 meV over 78–230 K; the dot redshifts ~6% slower than bulk
   GaAs — consistent with the paper's "practically identical".
7. **Varshni InP vs Laferrière's published wavelengths** (1301.28 → 1397.8 nm,
   4 → 300 K): computed 70.7 meV vs measured 65.8 meV (7%, class-level).
8. **Chatzarakis 78 K bound from measured inputs only** (S2 linewidths, digitized
   window, no fit parameters): cascade leakage g²₀ = 0.008 ≤ published 0.02. ✔
9. **Three independent routes to ρ(230 K) agree within 0.011**: joint fit
   (0.884), measured-Γ F2 inversion of the published g² = 0.36 (0.879), and
   the lifetime-ratio route from the printed S4 decay times (0.891). Refined
   decomposition at 230 K with measured Γ: ≈48% cascade leakage, ≈52%
   background (supersedes the earlier fit-only ~1/3 / ~2/3 estimate).
10. **Thermal magnitude**: bare 8 µm aperture on GaAs gives 1.1 K/mW —
    inside the published VCSEL-class 1–3 K/mW.

## Two discrepancies found (in the foundation doc, not the code)

- **F7 ρ_req row erratum**: the table states ρ ≥ 0.84–0.86 at Δ = 5.4; the
  correct values from the table's own ε row (0.27–0.30) are **0.825–0.843**
  (0.86 would require ε = 0.32). All FSIM maps use the recomputed values.
  Direction unchanged; the requirement is ~0.02 *easier* than the doc states.
- **Varshni-walk figure**: the doc's "~35–50 nm from 20→300 K" is low; the
  computed walk at 931 nm is ~59 nm (78→300 K) / ~67 nm (20→300 K), and the
  Chatzarakis paper's own "~50 nm" remark is the closer figure. The tracking
  rule is unaffected (it uses the Varshni form, not the nm figure), but the
  doc's number should be revised upward.
