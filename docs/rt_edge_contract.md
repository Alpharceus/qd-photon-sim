# Room-temperature edge-emitter contract

This is the opt-in contract for the room-temperature electrical edge-emitter tier. Legacy cards retain their existing behavior unless they explicitly select a new mode. Literature anchors live in `verify/data/rt_edge_anchors.yaml`; `[V]` means verified published value, `[DR]` derived from published values, `[E]` class estimate, and `[A]` assumption.

## Block schema

The new device fields are `dot.linewidth` (`"class"` is legacy; `"anchored"` opts into the anchor-selected linewidth), `ret.mode` (`"proxy"` is legacy; `"confinement"` opts into confinement retention), and `drive.mode` (`"EL"` or `"PL"` remain valid; `"EL-transport"` opts into transport-aware electrical drive). The new `emission` block has `type`: `"none"` (legacy) or `"edge"`. `aperture.compose` is boolean and defaults to `False`; `filter.track_material` defaults to `""`, meaning legacy hard-coded GaAs filtering; `drive.cw` defaults to `False`. Every stated default must reproduce the legacy path.

## Units and normalization

Energies are meV unless a field ends in `_eV`; temperatures are K; currents are uA; rates are ns^-1; lengths are nm except fields ending `_um` or `_um2`.

`b_e` is background photon counts inside the detection window per collected X photon, evaluated at the operating current and junction temperature. This reconciles the historical meanings: the `device.py` Arrhenius amplitude is first evaluated as its legacy background channel and then normalized to collected X counts; the card phrase “total at op point” is already that ratio; and the `spec.py` raw quantity must be converted to that ratio before use. Thus the legacy Arrhenius channel maps to `b_e = B_arrhenius(I,T_j)/S_X,collect(I,T_j)`; it is not an independently added background term.

## Temperature and rate mapping

`thermal.T_hs` is the heatsink set point. `T_j` is the self-consistent junction temperature and is the temperature consumed by anchored linewidth, retention/escape, electrical transport, and CW escape/refill. `T_hs` remains the legacy input only when the legacy thermal path has no self-heating calculation. Rate inputs and outputs use ns^-1; no implicit ps^-1 conversion is permitted.

## Aperture assumptions

The aperture-derived competitor count is continuous in the new composed model: its excess contribution produces a continuous g2 penalty, so changing area or density cannot create integer discontinuities. Integer rounding is legacy-only, retained solely when `aperture.compose: false`. Composition combines the aperture selection penalty with the normalized in-window background once, rather than treating the competitor count as a second collection loss.

## Lemma 1

**Lemma 1.** Beta factor, out-coupling, and collection efficiency are counted exactly once, in brightness. They must never lower g2, except through the existing spectral-leakage epsilon path. In particular, changing `emission.type` from `none` to `edge` changes collected brightness, not intrinsic multiphoton probability.

## Acceptance gates

The headline metric is pulsed intrinsic `g2(0)`. `g2_cw0` and `g2_cw0_raw` are secondary diagnostics: reported per-row and as coverage fractions, but they never gate PASS. Non-cryogenic operation means a thermoelectrically cooled package with heat-sink temperature `T_hs >= 230 K` (no cryogen). As requested in `../_goal/GOAL_PROMPT.md`, “Update 2026-09-06”, PASS requires that at least one `T_hs` in `{230, 250, 273, 300}` K has a physically eligible corner (collected pulsed flux >= 1 kHz) whose pulsed intrinsic `g2(0)` is below 0.5, plus every paper gate (evidence completeness and the hallucination self-test) to pass. The verdict reports `T_pass_min`, per-temperature headline coverage, and retains the 300 K result as the “room-temperature” line. Any missing-evidence anchor blocks PASS. A corner relying on `[A]` or `[E]` inputs must be tagged as such in its report, even if its numerical metric is favorable.

The pulsed g2 sub-result is IRF-independent; `headline_pass` also requires CW eligibility, which is IRF-convolved, so the deduplicated count is computed with `all()` over the IRF axis and any disagreement is reported as `headline_dedup_mismatch_groups`. The VERDICT line carries three coverage tokens: `headline_coverage` (row-based, kept for backward compatibility, identical to `rows_scheduled`), `rows_scheduled` (the raw scheduled-grid count, which repeats each (card, delta_xx, gamma300, lever, T_hs) combination once per irf_ps sample and so is not itself a coverage fraction), and `headline_coverage_pulsed` (deduplicated: one count per (card, delta_xx, gamma300, lever, T_hs) combination, via the `all()` reduction above).

## Evidence status

The YAML table is the machine-readable evidence ledger. The temperature trend, GaAs p-i-n I-V anchors, vertical-reference extraction efficiencies, InP/GaInP X-XX proxy, the 300 K linewidth class, and the InP/AlGaInP retention activation energy now have two independent verified sources each. The 300 K linewidth class rests on Matsuda et al., Phys. Rev. B 63, 121304(R) (2001) (12 meV, In0.5Ga0.5As/GaAs single dot) and Chatzarakis et al., Phys. Rev. Applied 20, 034011 (2023) (6-7 meV, InAs/GaAs (211)B single dot, the (211)B/(100) linewidth curves merging at T >= 250 K -- not an ensemble or mesa/microcavity-averaged number); both remain class proxies, not InP/GaAsP measurements. Laferriere et al.'s 16 meV entry is not part of this pair: it is the 300 K detection-bandpass *filter* width (an instrument upper bound), not a measured linewidth, and is tagged `[E]`/`status: missing` accordingly. The retention activation energy adds Schulz et al., physica status solidi (c) 6, 906 (2009) (150 meV, ensemble Arrhenius fit, Table 1 structure (a), reference InP/Al0.20Ga0.80InP barrier) alongside Bommer et al., J. Appl. Phys. 110, 063108 (2011) (96 +/- 7 meV, single-dot). The InP/GaInP X-XX proxy is re-sourced to its actual primary: the 4-6 meV range is Beirne et al., Phys. Rev. B 75, 195302 (2007), quoted (not independently measured) in Reischle et al. 2008 p. 12774; paired with Bommer et al. 2011 (7 +/- 1 meV), the union across both independent sources is 4-8 meV, overlapping only marginally near 6 meV.

The 80 K electrical g2 still lacks a second independent 80 K electrical single-dot measurement: Reischle et al., Appl. Phys. Lett. 97, 143513 (2010) is the only other electrically driven InP-dot g2 paper in the fact-checked digests, but it reports 20-40 K (Joule-heated), not 80 K, so it cannot honestly stand in for the missing anchor. The HKUST InP/GaAsP wavelength as a single-photon anchor now has a confirmed, DOI-sourced second wavelength candidate -- Luo et al., Optics Express 30, 40750 (2022), 750 nm InP/GaAsP QD laser on Si -- and that anchor's own status is `verified` (the underlying literature fact is genuine and DOI-sourced), but the *claim* still needs two distinct verified sources and has only this one: the primary anchor (gu25-hkust-wavelength, Gu et al. 2025, 755 nm) is kept `missing` because no independent matching single-dot evidence exists for it. The gaasp design card uses its confinement-derived `emission.lambda_nm: 815.7` nm, not the old 668 nm tabulation point; this is a known model deviation from the published 750 nm-class values of Gu 2025 / Luo 2022 (about 9% high). The X-XX entries are explicitly proxies: the required InP/GaAsP single-dot measurement remains absent.

Six anchors cite papers outside the fact-checked six-paper digest set (Gu 2025, Luo 2022, Matsuda 2001, Bommer 2011 x2, Schulz 2009): each carries a `verification` field recording how its value was independently confirmed (`digest`, `pdf:<path>`, `web:<url>`, or `unverified` -- an anchor cannot be `status: verified` while `verification: unverified`). `verify/data/rt_edge_digest_values.yaml` is a second, independently hand-transcribed value table (never derived from the anchors ledger) that `verify/verify_rt_edge_papers.py` cross-checks every verified anchor's value/unit/DOI against, and a DOI-prefix plausibility check (registered publisher prefixes, or an explicit `web:` whitelist) -- together these catch a fabricated anchor or a mutated real value that purely structural checks (unit, context tokens, tag validity) cannot.
